About Us

Escobar Property Services is a family owned local company serving Metro Atlanta since 2018. We provide reliable exterior cleaning, painting, and property maintenance for homeowners, HOAs, and property managers across the area.

We serve communities throughout Metro Atlanta including Lilburn, Duluth, Norcross, Lawrenceville, Alpharetta, Marietta, and surrounding areas. Whether it is a single home or a multi unit property, our focus is simple: deliver consistent, high quality results every time.

Our team is fully insured and equipped with professional grade tools to handle everything from pressure washing and gutter cleaning to painting and exterior maintenance. We take pride in showing up on time, doing the job right, and leaving every property looking its best.

We understand what property managers and homeowners need: fast response, clear communication, and dependable service. That is why many of our clients trust us for ongoing maintenance and repeat work.

If you are looking for a company that is easy to work with and delivers professional results, we are ready to help.
