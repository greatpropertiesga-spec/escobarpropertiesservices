High-pressure washing can crack siding, force water behind your walls, and strip paint, especially on older homes. Soft washing uses low pressure combined with professional-grade biodegradable solutions to kill algae, mold, and mildew at the root, not just blast it off the surface. The result lasts 4 to 6 times longer than pressure washing alone.

What's Included

  • Full exterior house washing (siding, trim, eaves)
  • Algae, mold, mildew, and green stain removal
  • Roof washing, safe for asphalt shingles
  • Fences, decks, and painted wood surfaces
  • Biodegradable, plant-safe cleaning solutions
  • Before-and-after walkthrough with homeowner

When You Need This Service

  • Green or black staining on siding
  • Dark streaks on roof shingles
  • Preparing to paint your home's exterior
  • Annual or bi-annual home maintenance
